  - { qty: 4, unit: "tbsp", item: "butter" }
  - { qty: 4, unit: "cloves", item: "garlic, thinly sliced" }
  - { qty: 0.25, unit: "tsp", item: "red pepper flakes" }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "cup", item: "grated parmesan" }
  - { qty: 2, unit: "tbsp", item: "chopped parsley" }
  - { qty: "", unit: "", item: "salt and black pepper, to taste" }
steps:
  - "Cook the spaghetti in well-salted boiling water until al dente. Save 1 cup of pasta water before draining."
  - "Meanwhile, melt the butter in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic and pepper flakes and cook until fragrant and just golden, about 2 minutes."
  - "Add the drained pasta and ½ cup pasta water. Toss until glossy."
  - "Add the parmesan and toss again, splashing in more pasta water if it looks dry."
  - "Season with salt and pepper, top with parsley and serve right away."
---
Don't let the garlic get dark brown. Golden is perfect, darker gets bitter.
