  - { qty: 2, unit: "", item: "eggs" }

  - { qty: 2, unit: "cups", item: "chocolate chips" }
steps:
  - "Melt the butter in a light-colored pan over medium heat, swirling, until it foams and smells nutty with brown bits on the bottom, 5–7 minutes. Pour into a bowl and cool 10 minutes."
  - "Whisk both sugars into the browned butter, then the eggs and vanilla."
  - "Stir in the flour, baking soda and salt, then fold in the chocolate chips."
  - "Chill the dough for 30 minutes."
  - "Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Scoop 2-tablespoon balls onto lined sheets, 2 inches apart."
  - "Bake 10–12 minutes, until the edges are golden and the centers still look soft. Cool on the sheet 5 minutes."
