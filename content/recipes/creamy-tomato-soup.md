steps:
  - "Melt the butter in a pot over medium heat. Cook the onion until soft, about 6 minutes, then add the garlic for 1 minute."
  - "Add the tomatoes with their juice, the broth and the sugar. Simmer 20 minutes, breaking up the tomatoes."
  - "Blend until smooth (carefully!) with an immersion blender or in batches."
  - "Stir in the cream, season with salt and pepper, and top with torn basil."
