  - { qty: "", unit: "", item: "pinch of salt" }

steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line an 8×8-inch pan with parchment."
  - "Mix the butter, powdered sugar, 1 cup flour and salt into a soft dough. Press it evenly into the pan."
  - "Bake the crust 18–20 minutes, until lightly golden."
  - "Whisk the eggs, sugar, 2 tbsp flour, lemon juice and zest until smooth. Pour over the hot crust."
  - "Bake 18–20 minutes more, until the filling is set with no jiggle."
  - "Cool completely, chill for an hour, then dust with powdered sugar and cut into 16 squares."
