  - { qty: 1, unit: "", item: "egg" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "vanilla extract" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "baking soda" }
  - { qty: 0.25, unit: "tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { qty: 1.5, unit: "cups", item: "all-purpose flour" }
steps:
  - "Heat the oven to 350°F (175°C) and line a 12-cup muffin tin."
  - "Mash the bananas in a bowl, then stir in the melted butter."
  - "Mix in the brown sugar, egg and vanilla."
  - "Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the batter and stir, then fold in the flour until no dry streaks remain."
  - "Divide the batter between the cups and bake 18–22 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean."

The spottier the bananas, the sweeter the muffins.
