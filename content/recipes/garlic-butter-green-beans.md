steps:
  - "Boil the green beans in salted water for 3–4 minutes, until bright green and crisp-tender. Drain."
  - "Melt the butter in a large skillet over medium heat and cook the garlic for 30 seconds."
  - "Add the beans and toss for 2 minutes to coat."
  - "Squeeze over the lemon, season with salt and pepper, and serve."
