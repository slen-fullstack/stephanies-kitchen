  - { qty: 1, unit: "tsp", item: "baking powder" }
  - { qty: 0.5, unit: "tsp", item: "baking soda" }
  - { qty: 0.25, unit: "tsp", item: "salt" }
  - { qty: 0.75, unit: "cup", item: "whole-milk ricotta" }
  - { qty: 0.75, unit: "cup", item: "milk" }
  - { qty: 2, unit: "", item: "eggs" }
  - { qty: 1, unit: "", item: "lemon, zested and juiced" }
  - { qty: 2, unit: "tbsp", item: "melted butter, plus more for the pan" }
steps:
  - "Whisk the flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda and salt in a large bowl."
  - "In another bowl, whisk the ricotta, milk, eggs, lemon zest, 1 tbsp lemon juice and melted butter until smooth."
  - "Fold the wet ingredients into the dry until just combined. A few lumps are good!"
  - "Heat a skillet over medium-low heat and add a little butter."
  - "Pour about ¼ cup batter per pancake. Cook until bubbles form and the edges look set, about 2–3 minutes, then flip and cook 1–2 minutes more."
  - "Serve warm with a pat of butter and maple syrup or berries."
---
Keep the heat on the low side. Ricotta batter browns fast and needs a minute to cook through.
